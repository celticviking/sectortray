Lay one of each utensil flat and measure it with your tape. Length is tip to tip. Handle is across the end of the handle. Head is across the widest part of the bowl, tines or blade. Each type gets its own slot, tapered from head to handle. Slots alternate head-to-tail so they pack tightly.
