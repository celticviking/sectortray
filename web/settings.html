Headroom Space left between the tallest bin and the top of the drawer, so the drawer closes without scraping.Kept free under the drawer top
mm
Joint clearance Gap left on each side of the snap clips and bow-tie keys that join a bin printed in pieces. Too tight to push together: raise it a little (0.05 mm at a time). Loose: lower it. Print the joint test to check.For bins printed in pieces, per side
mm
Joint test printTwo small 2 × 1 bins cut in half: one with snap clips, one with a bow-tie key. Under an hour to print. Clips are tested on a Prusa CORE One; keys are still experimental. Try this before printing a big bin in pieces.
Slicer The program Open in… sends your parts to, as a 3MF file with every part named and laid out on the bed. Only PrusaSlicer has been tried for real; it can also hold several beds in one project. The others should open the same file; if parts land off the bed, use that slicer's Arrange.

New bins

Starting point for bins you add
Label tab A little shelf along the back wall for a written or printed label. Useful in a screw drawer; usually not needed for kitchen or bedroom drawers. A drawer can turn tabs on for all its bins (Bins page), and each bin can still change its own.Off: new bins have no label tab unless the drawer says so
Stacking lip A small rim around the top so another bin can sit on top. Off saves about 3.5 mm of height; bins without it can't be stacked.Off: new bins have a plain top edge
Finger scoopCurved front inside, for sliding small things out
Print too-big bins in pieces When a bin is bigger than your printer bed, it's set to print in pieces that join together (snap clips) as soon as it gets too big. You can still change or turn that off on the Bins page.Automatically, as soon as a bin is too big for the bed
