Height u is Gridfinity’s height unit: 1u = 7 mm. A 3u bin is 21 mm tall, plus the stacking lip. Label tabs need at least 3u. ·
Print in pieces The bin is cut on the grid lines into pieces that fit your printer bed. Each piece still sits in whole baseplate squares, so the grid keeps them lined up.
Joint Snap clips: a thin plate on the inside of each wall reaches past the seam and grips the other piece. Bow-tie keys: small keys you print separately and press into pockets in the floor across the seam (a drop of glue makes it permanent). None: the pieces just sit side by side in the grid.

Features

Label tab A label tab is a little shelf along the back wall where you can stick or write a label. It’s readable from above. Bins under 3u skip it.Shelf along the back wall
Finger scoop A finger scoop is a curved ramp inside the front wall. It lets you slide small things (screws, teaspoons) out with one finger instead of pinching.Curved front for scooping
Stacking lip A stacking lip is a small rim around the top edge. Another bin can sit on top of it. Turn it off to gain about 3.5 mm of height in a shallow drawer.Adds about 3.5 mm on top
Magnet holes Pockets in the bin’s feet for 6 × 2 mm disc magnets. They match the baseplate’s magnet holes, so the bin stays put.Matches the baseplate
Advanced 3 options
Label tab style Auto sizes a tab per compartment (up to one square wide). Full runs the whole width. Left, Center and Right place a short tab.
Screw holes Holes in the feet for M3 screws. Use them if the baseplate is screwed down and bins should be too.For screwing bins down
Half-grid feet Splits each 42 mm foot into four 21 mm feet, so the bin also fits half-grid plates. Magnets go in the corners only.Fits 21 mm half-grid plates
