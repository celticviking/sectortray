Baseplate

Magnet holes Round pockets in each square for 6 × 2 mm disc magnets. Bins with matching magnets snap down and won’t slide when the drawer slams.6 × 2 mm, 4 per square
Plate style Standard: thin floor with cut-outs and magnet pockets (9.35 mm tall).
Lite: open frame, no floor, fastest print, no magnets (5 mm).
Weighted: thick floor with pockets for steel weights, so the plate stays put without glue (11.4 mm).
Grid size Standard: 42 mm Gridfinity squares, so bins fit any Gridfinity baseplate; the leftover becomes a filler strip. Fit to drawer: squares a little smaller or bigger than 42 mm so the grid fills the whole drawer with no border. Bins made for a fitted drawer only fit that drawer (and others with the same square size).
Grid position Gridfinity squares are 42 mm, so a drawer rarely fits them exactly. The leftover becomes a flat filler strip. Centered splits it between both sides; pick a side to push the bins tight against that wall and put all the filler on the other side.
