## Gridfinity

Gridfinity is the open modular storage system created by Zack Freedman (MIT license). See [gridfinity.xyz](https://gridfinity.xyz/), it's cool! SectorTray is an independent tool for designing Gridfinity drawers entirely offline; it's neither made by, nor affiliated with, the Gridfinity project.
